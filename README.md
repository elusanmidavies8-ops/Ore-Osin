## Overview

Smart Paws is a community-driven web platform designed to bring together pet owners, animal lovers, and people looking to provide a safe and loving home for a pet. The idea is simple: create a welcoming digital space where people can connect, share valuable insights, celebrate their pets, and support responsible animal care.

Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, Smart Paws combines a lightweight front-end foundation with a user-friendly experience that can grow as the platform evolves.

## Mission

To build a trusted, inclusive, and helpful online community where pet owners and animal lovers can connect, learn from one another, and support better outcomes for pets and families alike.

## Why Smart Paws?

Pets are more than companions — they are part of the family. Smart Paws is designed to make it easier for people to:

- connect with others who share a love for animals,
- share stories, experiences, and advice,
- discover useful pet-care content,
- support responsible and informed pet ownership,
- help pets find safe, loving homes through adoption or rehoming opportunities.

## About the Project

Smart Paws is built to create a meaningful social experience for people who care about animals. It allows users to explore community content, engage with others, and build a stronger network around pet care and adoption support.

The platform focuses on accessibility, warmth, and community-driven interaction, with a modern user experience that is easy to navigate and expand over time.

## Key Features

### Community & Social Experience
- Personalized home feed based on followed accounts and interests
- User profiles for building community relationships
- Pet profiles for sharing details, stories, and photos
- Community posts for updates, questions, and daily moments
- Comments and discussions for advice and engagement

### Discovery & Engagement
- Follow system to stay connected with pet owners and communities
- Discover new people, pets, and content relevant to user interests
- Encourage interaction through meaningful, community-first engagement

### Adoption & Rehoming Support
- Pet rehoming notices to help connect pets with suitable homes
- Adoption-focused listings that make it easier for pets to find caring families

## Tech Stack

Smart Paws is currently built using:

- HTML for structure and content
- CSS for modern, responsive styling and layout
- JavaScript for interactivity and dynamic user experiences

This front-end stack keeps the project lightweight, accessible, and easy to expand as additional features are introduced.

## Who Is Smart Paws For?

Smart Paws is designed for:

- pet owners who want to connect with others,
- animal lovers who enjoy learning about pet care and community life,
- people interested in responsible pet ownership,
- individuals looking to adopt a pet or provide a loving home.

You do not need to own a pet to be part of the community — everyone who cares about animals is welcome.

## Product Vision

To become a trusted and welcoming hub for pet communities, where connection, knowledge-sharing, and responsible ownership come together in one seamless experience.

Smart Paws is currently in active development. Features, priorities, and technical decisions may evolve as the platform grows and the community expands.
