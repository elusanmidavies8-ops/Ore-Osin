# Ore Osin

**A community for pet owners and animal lovers, and a safe path to responsible pet rehoming.**

## What the name means

*Ọ̀rẹ́ Ọ̀sìn* (written Ore Osin without tone marks) is a Yoruba word. *Ọ̀rẹ́* means **friend** and *ọ̀sìn* means **pet** (a domestic animal). Together the name reads as **friend of pets**.

The tone-marked form is used in the logo and formal text. Plain Ore Osin is used in running text, file names and web addresses.

It states what the project is for: a place where people who love animals befriend one another, share what they know and help pets find caring homes. (It replaces the earlier working name, Smart Paws.)

## What it is

Ore Osin is a responsive website that brings pet owners and animal lovers into one community. You do not need to own a pet to join.

It is **not** an online pet shop or a buying-and-selling marketplace. Its purpose is community, shared knowledge and responsible rehoming.

## Status

**Planning.** The requirements are written (Software Requirements Specification, version 0.2, draft). Build has not started. Nothing here can be run yet.

## Planned features

- Accounts with email and password, and a profile (name, photo, bio, city, optional pets)
- Posts with text, up to 4 images and topic tags
- Flat comments and likes on posts
- One-way following of other members
- A personalised feed from the members you follow and the tags you choose, newest first
- Search and browsing by tag
- Rehoming listings (pet details, health and vaccination notes, reason, city, 1 to 5 photos, status)
- One-to-one text chat, with block and report
- Reports and admin moderation, plus a rule that listings may not ask for payment

## Release plan

1. **Release 1, community core:** accounts, profiles, posts, comments, likes, following, feed, discovery, reporting and admin removal.
2. **Release 2, rehoming and chat:** listings, rehoming discovery and one-to-one chat, released together because a listing needs a way to contact its owner.

## Out of scope for now

Online shop, carts, checkout and payments; a marketplace; group chat and media in chat; push notifications; groups within the platform; nested comment replies; advanced recommendation algorithms; identity verification; formal adoption approval and tracking; mobile apps.
