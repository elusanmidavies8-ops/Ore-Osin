# Smart Paws 🐾

**Connecting pet owners. Sharing experiences. Finding loving homes.**

Smart Paws is a community-driven platform designed to connect pet owners and animal lovers. It provides a space where people can build relationships, share experiences, exchange pet-care advice and help pets find new homes through responsible rehoming.

## 🎯 Project Vision

To build a welcoming online community where pet owners and animal lovers can connect, learn from one another and support responsible pet ownership.

## ✨ Planned Features

- **Personalised Home Feed:** Discover posts based on followed accounts and user interests.
- **User Profiles:** Create personal profiles and connect with other members.
- **Pet Profiles:** Share information, photos and experiences about pets.
- **Community Posts:** Publish updates, photos, questions and experiences.
- **Comments and Discussions:** Interact with other members and exchange advice.
- **Follow System:** Follow pet owners and discover their content.
- **Pet Rehoming:** Create rehoming notices and connect interested people with current pet owners.

## 👥 Who Is Smart Paws For?

- Pet owners who want to connect with others.
- People interested in learning about pets and responsible pet care.
- Individuals looking to adopt or provide a new home for a pet.

You do not need to own a pet to be part of the community.
